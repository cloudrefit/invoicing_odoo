Seamless Saudi E-Invoicing Phase 2

Automate your Phase 2 ZATCA e-invoicing workflow. This plugin connects your Odoo instance to the CloudRefit ZATCA Gateway for automatic signing, reporting, and clearance.

  • 4-Step Onboarding Wizard
  • B2B Clearance & B2C Reporting
  • Automatic QR Code Generation

  • معالج ربط سهل من 4 خطوات
  • تخليص B2B وتبليغ B2C
